Oregon State National Historic District

Oregon State University’s campus was designated a National Historic District in 2008.

I always felt like this would be a fantastic data to explore and showcase some of the great buildings found on campus. While you explore the map, click on an orange point of interest pin to show more detail of what the name of OSU was when the building was built.

  • Benton Hall, built in 1889 during the existence of Oregon Agricultural College (OAC)
  • Cordley Hall, built in 1956 during the existence of Oregon State College (OSC)
  • The Valley Library, built in 1962 during the existence of Oregon State University

This map uses data from the OSU Foundation and OSU Facilities, which made the original map.
